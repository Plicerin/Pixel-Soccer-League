# Bang for Buck — Stadium Polish Ideas

1. **Floodlight pylons** — 4 tall light towers at the corners with emissive tips. Huge visual payoff, especially with the night preset.
2. **Corner flags** — small poles with animated cloth at the 4 pitch corners. Classic soccer detail.
3. **Stadium roof/canopy** — partial overhang above the top tier. Turns flat bleachers into a real stadium silhouette.
4. **Mowing stripes** — alternating light/dark grass bands across the field. Very recognizable. ⬅ NEXT
5. **Camera flashes on goals** — brief random white flickers in the crowd when a goal is scored.
6. **Crowd wave** — Mexican wave that propagates around the stands on goals or randomly.
7. **Goal nets** — visible mesh behind the goalposts (may already exist — haven't checked).
8. **Weather particles** — rain drops / snow flakes when weather is set to RAINY/SNOW.
